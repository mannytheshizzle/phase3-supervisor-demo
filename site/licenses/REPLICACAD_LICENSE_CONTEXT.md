The two supplied assets are unmodified GLBs from installed revision
`6be07e532c83c5799a6e96152f926d07838c2084` of
[ai-habitat/ReplicaCAD_dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai-habitat/ReplicaCAD_dataset).

and says **CC BY-NC 4.0**, copyright Facebook, Inc. and its affiliates.
Per-file source and shipped hashes are in `ASSET_PROVENANCE.json`.

Checked 10 November 2026: the [official project page](https://aihabitat.org/datasets/replica_cad/)
and current [official dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai-habitat/ReplicaCAD_dataset)
say **CC BY 4.0**. These statements differ; this demo does not assume that the
newer statement replaces the supplied revision's notice.

modification disclosures and the supplied licence. The installed CC BY-NC terms
expressly permit attributed non-commercial redistribution; a public website is
not inherently a commercial use. No ads, sale, recruitment or commercial service
